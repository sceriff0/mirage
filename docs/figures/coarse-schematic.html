Schematic 5 · coarse alignment

STARE's coarse anchor: a rotation sweep of the normalised cross-correlation estimates one global pose per moving slide, and its residual — not its speed — is what the per-tile step depends on

1 · pipeline 2 · registration 3 · quality control 4 · lazy reads 5 · coarse alignment 6 · accuracy measures

The STARE (tiled) registration backend's first step — TILED_COARSE — estimates a single whole-slide rigid transform M0 on a downsampled thumbnail before the per-tile step refines the residual left over (the anchor stage of manuscript Fig. 2a). The front-end that produces that estimate is a rotation sweep of the normalised cross-correlation (NCC), with a keypoint + RANSAC fit kept only as a fallback; it replaced a learned DISK + LightGlue matcher on 2026-09-27. This figure grounds why that swap happened, what the code kept underneath it, why the memory request fell, and what is — and is not — verified about the anchor.

a

The naive expectation — a cheap thumbnail step, any detector would do

Coarse alignment looks like it only has to be fast: a whole-slide anchor computed on a small thumbnail, refined later. What it actually has to deliver is a residual narrow enough to land inside the range the per-tile step can recover — that requirement, not runtime, is what selects the method.

COARSE delivers
M0 + residual (px)
One 3×3 rigid matrix (rotation + translation) mapping moving → reference pixels, plus a residual estimate — estimate_rigid's return value.
the constraint
residual < capture range
After the anchor, the per-tile step recovers a displacement of up to about two strides per axis — ±128 px (~±42 µm at 0.325 µm/px) at the default reg_tiled_stride of 64. reg_tiled_halo (default 256 px) is the threshold this stage warns at and the default of the solve's range gate; it is not what a tile reads.
what enforces it
nothing
TILED_COARSE logs a warning once the residual reaches the halo, and flags an anchor it could not verify, but nothing downstream checks either and no task fails on it — a slide can mis-register silently and the run still exits 0.
Why this is a correctness requirement, not a speed one. A worse anchor does not merely take a different amount of time — it leaves a wider residual, and once that residual exceeds what the tile step recovers the per-tile step is measuring a shift to the wrong structure while the pipeline keeps running. The anchor is chosen for robustness, not for how quickly it runs.
b

Why the front-end changed, and what did not

A learned matcher (DISK + LightGlue) preceded this one, and three classical front-ends behind a selector parameter preceded that. The learned matcher was retired on 2026-09-27 and removed from the image — not kept behind a flag. The deterministic match-to-transform fit and the full-resolution lift underneath were kept.

changed — the front-end
the anchorretired & replaced
What estimates the rotation and translation between the two thumbnails. The module's own docstring says its only job is to be robust: "a precise-but-fragile matcher is the wrong tool, and this module does not use one". At 256 px the sweep matches tissue shape, which survives nuclei turnover, tissue loss and a gamma change.
kept — the fit
estimate_transform_from_matcheskept
RANSAC + model fit over point correspondences — now the core of the keypoint fallback only — plus scale_transform_to_full_res, the thumbnail-to-full-resolution lift.
Why tissue shape and not point correspondences. Reference and moving planes come from different imaging rounds: nuclei turn over, tissue is lost and exposure differs, so two views of the same tissue can match each other far worse than they should, with no error raised. On the change's own hard synthetic pairs (80% nuclei turnover, 25% tissue loss, gamma 0.6, five rotations) the sweep recovered 10 of 10, where DISK + LightGlue recovered 2 of 10 on the same 512 px pairs (CHANGELOG.md).
c

The mechanism — from two raw planes to one lifted transform

Six steps, run once per moving slide (manuscript Methods, "STARE"; Supplementary Note 3.2).

1normalize_intensity + mask
Raw decimated plane → rescaled to [0, 1] over a p1–p99.9 percentile window (_NORM_PCT), blurred with a Gaussian of σ = 1 px and set to zero outside an Otsu tissue mask — a correctness fix for the cross-round exposure mismatch in panel b, and what keeps background out of the correlation.
2rotation sweep at 256 px
Both planes reduced to a longest side of 256 px; the moving one is rotated over 360° in 3° steps and every angle is scored by the peak of the normalised cross-correlation. At this size the sweep scores the tissue outline rather than individual nuclei.
3refine on the full thumbnail
The two best distinct angles are refined on the full thumbnail (longest side ≤ reg_tiled_coarse_max_dim, 1024 px) over ±3° in 1° steps and then ±0.75° in 0.25° steps; the translation is placed to sub-pixel precision by tenfold-upsampled cross-correlation.
4acceptance gates
M0 is accepted when its correlation peak is at least 0.3 and at least 1.15× that of the runner-up.
5fallback, then full-resolution patches
Otherwise estimate_transform_from_matches(src, dst) fits a euclidean transform (rotation + translation, 3 DOF) to keypoint matches by RANSAC, accepted with at least 30 inliers. If neither passes, the candidates are compared on up to eight 1024 px nuclear patches read at full resolution: one confirmed by at least three patches is used, otherwise the best candidate is kept and flagged unverified.
6lift to full resolution
scale_transform_to_full_res multiplies the translation column by the decimation factor (rotation is scale-invariant and passes through unchanged). Both COARSE outputs — M0 and the reported coarse_tre — are full-resolution numbers from this point on; for the sweep coarse_tre is the search grid's quantisation bound and n_inliers is 0.
d

The memory shape — a few thumbnail-sized canvases, not a U-Net's activations

The anchor holds a few FFT canvases of the thumbnail, so its memory is small and is set by reg_tiled_coarse_max_dim, never by the slide's own dimensions. That is what let the request fall from 48 GB to 2 GB.

measured peak, whole stage
NCC sweep + refineunder 0.5 GB
0.25 GB with a 512 px thumbnail, 0.43 GB at 1024 px and 0.40 GB at 2048 px — interpreter, imports, banded reads and the anchor together. TILED_COARSE's memory closure asks 2 × (0.5 + 0.1 × Mpx) GB floored at 2 GB — 2 GB at the 1024 px default — times task.attempt.
what it replaced
DISK + LightGlueretired 2026-09-27
A U-Net whose activation memory scaled with thumbnail area (GB ≈ 1.1 + 7.3 × Mpx, about 32 GB at 2048 px), for which this stage asked 48 GB. torch and kornia are no longer in the STARE image, and its smoke test asserts that torch is not importable.
What reg_tiled_coarse_max_dim buys now. It is the refinement resolution — the sweep always runs at 256 px — and its cost is CPU time, not memory. A 256 px floor is enforced at launch, before any task starts: anything below it — 0 included, which used to mean "no decimation", i.e. the full-resolution plane — aborts the run.
e

The knobs

Defaults verbatim from the params{} block of nextflow.config, the only place a default is declared. reg_tiled_coarse_max_dim and reg_tiled_halo carry plain defaults and are set directly (the reg_tiled_mode cost tiers were removed; passing it is refused at launch unless it is high).

reg_tiled_coarse_max_dim1024 anchor refinement thumbnail, longest side (px) — the knob from panel d. 256 px floor enforced at launch.
reg_tiled_halo256 anchor warning threshold + range-gate default (px), panel a — not a tile read margin.
f

Honest limits

Three things this anchor does not have behind it, stated plainly rather than left for a reviewer to discover.

1 — no head-to-head measurement on tissue The change's record compares the sweep with the learned matcher on ten hard synthetic pairs only. On tissue the manuscript scores the anchor as a stage of STARE, not against another anchor: rigid alignment by M0 alone gave a median matched-pair nuclear Dice of 0.57 on the 64 cohort round pairs, against 0.84 after the tile stage (manuscript Supplementary Table 2).
2 — an unverified anchor does not fail the run When neither the correlation gates, the keypoint fallback nor the full-resolution patches confirm a candidate, the best one is used, flagged (coarse_trusted false in the M0 JSON) and warned about. The slide can then come out mis-registered with exit 0, so that flag and the registration QC are the only signal.
3 — the residual is an estimate, not a per-pixel guarantee M0 is computed on the thumbnail and then lifted to full resolution by a single multiplicative scale; it is re-examined at full resolution only when the gates fail. For the sweep the reported residual is the search grid's quantisation bound, not a measured error. It is an estimate of the global pose error, not a guarantee about any individual full-resolution pixel.

Schematic 5. The STARE coarse front-end. (a) TILED_COARSE estimates one global rigid transform per moving slide on a shared thumbnail; the per-tile step that follows can only recover a residual of about two strides per axis, and nothing downstream checks that it did — a wide residual mis-registers a slide while the run still exits 0. (b) A learned matcher (DISK + LightGlue) was retired on 2026-09-27 and replaced by a rotation sweep of the normalised cross-correlation of the tissue outline; the match-to-transform RANSAC fit, now used only by the keypoint fallback, and the thumbnail-to-full-resolution lift were kept. (c) Six steps carry a plane from raw counts to a lifted transform: percentile normalisation with a tissue mask, a 256 px rotation sweep in 3° steps, refinement of the two best angles on the 1024 px thumbnail, acceptance gates on the correlation peak (0.3) and its ratio to the runner-up (1.15), a keypoint + RANSAC fallback with full-resolution patch verification, and a full-resolution lift. (d) The anchor holds a few thumbnail-sized canvases, so the whole stage peaks below 0.5 GB and requests 2 GB, where the U-Net it replaced asked 48 GB. (e) The two knobs governing this step carry plain defaults in nextflow.config and are set directly (the cost tiers were removed). (f) The anchor has no tissue accuracy comparison against another anchor, continues with a flagged, unverified anchor rather than failing, and reports a residual that is an estimate lifted from a thumbnail, not a full-resolution guarantee.
